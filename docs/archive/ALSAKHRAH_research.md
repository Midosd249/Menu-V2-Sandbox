## Menu integrity policy

No stable official current menu with reliable prices was found in the public sources reviewed. The case study therefore uses a small clearly labeled reference/prototype dataset based only on publicly named dishes: عش البلبل الحموي and برك اللحمه. Their prices and full descriptions remain unverified and are rendered as **السعر حسب الطلب / Price on request**. No logo, food imagery, opening hours, rating, or phone number is treated as canonical unless marked as public-source information requiring owner confirmation.
