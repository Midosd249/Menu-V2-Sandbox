# AL MAS public-source research

Research date: 2026-08-28.

## Verified from public sources

- Name/brand: AL MAS Family Restaurant / AL MAS Restaurant.
- Cuisine positioning: Indian (north and south), Arabic, and Chinese cuisines, according to the public Facebook page.
- Public Facebook address: Al Hawwari, Al Malaz, 53, Riyadh 12812, Saudi Arabia.
- Tripadvisor address: 53, Al Hawwari, Al Malaz near National Care Hospital, Riyadh 12812, Saudi Arabia.
- Tripadvisor map coordinates/link are publicly listed for the same location.
- Public phone numbers differ by source. Instagram lists +966 53 776 5212, +966 57 179 5093, +966 53 771 2037, and +966 55 248 6054. Facebook lists +966 57 123 0205. Tripadvisor lists +966 57 179 5093. The demo should use the Instagram primary number only with a source note, and should not imply the other numbers are canonical.
- Public social links: Instagram @almas_family_restaurant, Facebook /almasfamilyrestaurantksa.
- Public websites linked by sources: almasksa.com and almasrestaurent.com. They were not treated as verified menu data.
- Public hours: Tripadvisor explicitly says no hours are available. The demo must not invent opening hours.
- Public menu evidence: public Instagram posts show specific promotional dishes and prices for dated events, including Onam/sadya offerings, but these are time-bound promotional posts rather than a stable current menu. They should not be seeded as permanent products without a clear prototype label.
- Public review evidence: Tripadvisor describes Indian/South Indian food and mentions dishes such as masala dosa, biryani, chicken tikka masala, chilli paneer, and sadya in reviews. These are customer-review references, not an official current menu.

## Prototype policy

AL MAS is marked as a portfolio/prototype tenant. The public menu will use a small clearly labeled prototype dataset with no invented price claims. Product prices will be null/"السعر حسب التوفر" until the restaurant confirms a current menu. Opening hours will be shown as "غير منشورة — يرجى التواصل". The address and phone links will carry a visible "معلومات عامة من مصادر منشورة" context in the admin/readme, not a claim of an active client relationship.
